About Repurpose Textiles

We keep wearable clothing and usable textiles in circulation.

One Small Donation, One More Reason to Smile

At Repurpose Textiles, we believe that something you no longer need could be exactly what someone else is looking for. That’s the simple idea behind why we started.

Every day, perfectly usable clothes and other goods go to waste when they could have a second life with someone who truly needs them. We want to change that, one donation at a time.

But this journey isn’t just about clothing. It’s about people, communities, and the small acts of kindness that can make a real difference. We want to encourage more people to rethink what they throw away and discover the good their unused items can do.

And the best part? You don’t have to do something extraordinary to make a difference. Sometimes, all it takes is passing on something you no longer need.

To everyone who donates, supports us, spreads the word, or becomes a part of our journey, we’re genuinely grateful. You’re not just helping us grow a business; you’re helping us build a community that believes in giving things a second chance.

We’re just getting started, and we would love for you to be a part of this journey with us.

Give more. Waste less. Make a difference.

Our mission

At Repurpose Textiles, our mission is to reduce textile waste and promote a more sustainable future by giving unwanted clothing a chance to find new owners and continue serving a purpose.

Repurpose Textiles was created on the belief that environmental responsibility begins within our communities. Through community partnerships and responsible textile collection, we strive to keep reusable clothing out of landfills and extend its useful life. We are dedicated to giving back.

Our vision

Our vision extends beyond collecting textiles. We hope to build a network of communities and organizations working together to reduce unnecessary textile disposal, extend the useful life of existing garments and encourage more sustainable habits.

Because the most sustainable clothing may be the clothing that already exists.
