Who We Work With

We partner with businesses, organizations, and communities that share our commitment to sustainability and responsible textile reuse.

How It Works

  1. Connect With UsTell us about your organization, location, and collection requirements.
  2. Establish Your Collection ProgramTogether, we explore suitable collection arrangements, locations, and schedules.
  3. Bring Your Community TogetherEncourage employees, residents, customers, and community members to contribute their unwanted clothing and textiles.
  4. Give Textiles a New PurposeCollected materials are directed toward appropriate reuse, repurposing, and textile recovery opportunities.
